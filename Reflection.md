# Week 12 Practical Activity Reflection

I developed the Vehicle Fleet Management System using Dart to apply the programming concepts from our previous activities. I used classes and objects to represent different vehicles, including jeepneys, cars, and motorcycles. I applied encapsulation to protect passenger information and inheritance to reuse common vehicle properties and methods. I also used polymorphism, abstraction, and mixins to organize vehicle behaviors and GPS navigation. For asynchronous programming, I used `async`, `await`, and `Future` to perform vehicle diagnostics. This activity helped me understand how different Dart concepts work together in a practical application. I also improved my ability to organize code, handle conditions, and create reusable methods.
